Screen-by-Screen Walkthrough

Prototype Walkthrough

Explore every screen of Sakay Partido, from onboarding to ride completion, with detailed descriptions of each interface.

📱

The Sakay Partido prototype consists of 16 screens covering the complete user journey — authentication, ride booking, live tracking, payment, and emergency safety features. Each screen was designed for motorcycle and tricycle commuters in the Partido area, Camarines Sur. The walkthrough below is organized by user flow.

Flow 1 — Onboarding

🚀 Getting Started
Screen 01
Splash Screen
The app opens with a full-screen blue gradient and the Sakay Partido logo centered in a white circle. The tagline "Let's Go!" appears below, giving riders an energetic, welcoming first impression before the app loads.
Branding App Launch
Screen 02
Log In
Returning users enter their registered phone number and password. A "Remember me" checkbox reduces friction for daily commuters, while a "Forgot Password?" link ensures account recovery is always accessible. A "Sign up" link guides new users seamlessly.
Authentication Returning Users
Screen 03
Sign Up / Create Account
New users fill in their full name, phone number, an optional email, and a password. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy checkboxes ensure informed consent. Email is marked optional, lowering the barrier for users who only have a phone number.
Registration New Users
↓

Flow 2 — Booking a Ride

🗺️ Find & Book
Screen 04
Home Page
The home page shows a live map of the area with the user's current location pinned. A "Where to?" panel slides up from the bottom. The top bar displays the SOS button and notifications, while the bottom navigation provides quick access to Bookings, Places, Wallet, and Profile.
Live Map Navigation Hub SOS Access
Screen 05
Pick-Up & Drop-Off
Users confirm their pickup location (auto-detected as Current) and select a destination from their saved places or by typing. The "Saved Places" dropdown lets frequent commuters quickly pick Home, Work, or favorites, cutting booking time significantly.
Location Input Saved Places
Screen 06
Choose a Ride
Two ride options are presented: Motor (Door-to-Door) at ₱30 for solo riders with a 3-min wait, and Tricycle at ₱20 for 2–3 passengers with a 5-min wait. Distance and estimated travel time are shown at the top, helping users make an informed choice.
Fare Comparison
↓

Flow 3 — During the Ride

🛵 Live Ride Experience
Screen 07
Driver Found
A confirmation banner announces the matched driver. The rider sees the driver's name, star rating, trip count, and vehicle plate. Quick Call and Message buttons allow direct contact. Trip details (pickup, destination, fare) are summarized, and a Safety Features notice confirms live monitoring is active.
Driver Info SOS Active Safety Monitor
Screen 08
Ride in Progress
A live progress bar at the top tracks the journey in real time alongside a countdown timer. The map updates dynamically as the driver moves. Rider info, call/message actions, and fare remain visible throughout. The prominent SOS button stays accessible throughout the ride for immediate emergency response.
Live Tracking Progress Bar SOS
Screen 09
Arrived at Destination
A modal then presents three payment options: Cash, SakayPartido Wallet, or GCash(E-wallet). The total fare and route summary are prominently displayed, letting the rider confirm payment before the trip closes.
Ride Complete Cash Wallet GCash
Screen 10
Rate Your Driver
After payment, users rate their experience with the driver using a 5-star system. An optional text field captures additional feedback. A "Skip" button respects users who don't wish to rate, while "Submit Rating" contributes to the driver's reliability score on the platform.
Star Rating Feedback
Screen 11
Complete Ride Summary
The full trip is shown on the map with both pins visible (Goa → Lagonoy). Driver details, fare, and safety status are displayed one final time. A prominent green "Complete Ride" button closes the session and returns the user to the home screen ready for the next booking.
Trip Summary End Session
↓

Flow 4 — App Features

⚙️ Additional Screens
Screen 12
My Bookings
A tabbed view organizes trips into Active, Scheduled, and History. The active card shows the current ride's status (Ride in Progress), driver name, vehicle, pickup and destination, fare, and a live "Track Live" button, so users can monitor an ongoing trip without leaving the bookings tab.
Active Rides History Live Track
Screen 13
Saved Places
Users store frequently visited locations under Home, Work, Favorites, and Other categories. A search bar allows quick filtering across saved addresses. A floating "+" button lets users add new places at any time. Each entry has a context menu for editing or deleting.
Home & Work Favorites Search
Screen 14
Wallet
The wallet card displays the SakayPartido balance with Top Up and Withdraw quick actions. Monthly spending and savings versus regular fare are highlighted. Linked payment methods such as GCash, Cash, and PayMaya, are listed below. A promo banner advertises ₱50 off for top-ups of ₱200 or more.
Balance Savings Promo
Screen 15
Profile
The profile screen shows the user's avatar, verified badge, and member-since date. Personal info (phone, email) is displayed securely. Quick links to Payment Methods, Saved Places, Ride History, and Notifications make account management centralized and accessible in one place.
Verified Account Settings History
Screen 16
SOS Emergency Page
When the SOS button is triggered, the screen turns red with a clear "SOS ACTIVATED – Help is on the way" header. The activation time, live location sharing status, and siren state are shown. The user's exact GPS coordinates are displayed with a map link. Local emergency contacts, are immediately contacted.
SOS Active Live Location Emergency Contacts

Walkthrough Summary

📱

16 Screens

Full end-to-end prototype from splash screen to ride completion and emergency features.

🛵

2 Ride Types

Motor (door-to-door) and Tricycle (shared) are tailored to Partido commuters.

🔴

Safety-First Design

💳

3 Payment Methods

Cash, SakayPartido Wallet, and E-wallets, covering banked and unbanked users alike.
