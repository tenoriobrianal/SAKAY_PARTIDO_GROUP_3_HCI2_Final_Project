Group Reflections

What We Learned

Personal takeaways from each group member on usability, HCI, and the Sakay Partido evaluation experience.

💭

Each member of the Sakay Partido team shares their personal reflection on what they learned, what challenges they encountered, and how this study shaped their understanding of user-centered design.

Member Reflections

Brian Al Tenorio
Project Leader · Data Analyst / Documenter
"

Leading this project from the prototype stage all the way to usability testing was both challenging and rewarding. When we were building Sakay Partido, I was mostly focused on making sure everything was complete and functional, but during the actual testing, I realized how different it feels to watch real users interact with something we made. It made me understand that being the designer and being the user are two completely different perspectives. The usability testing phase taught me more about HCI than any lecture could, because the feedback was real, immediate, and honest.

Shejay Barranda
Member · UI/UX Designer
"

As the UI/UX designer of the group,I learned a lot about during the prototype creation up to the prototype usabiltity evaluation. I always focused on how the app looked. I learned that good design isn't about adding more; it's about removing what doesn't need to be there so users can get to what they need without thinking twice.

Mary Rose Oriana
Member · Prototype Presenter / Data Gatherer
"

Being the one who presented the prototype and gathered data during testing. It made me realize that data gathering isn't just about collecting scores, it's about observing real human behavior, and those small moments of confusion are just as important as any number on the SUS questionnaire.

Christine Joy Saor
Member · Documenter
"

Documenting the evaluation process made me pay really close attention to the details that are easy to overlook. I learned that consistency in HCI isn't just a design preference, it's a form of communication. When everything looks and behaves the same way, users feel confident. When it doesn't, they start to second-guess themselves.

Our Collective Reflection

Group 3 — Sakay Partido Team
Brian · Shejay · Mary Rose · Christine
"

As a group, this project taught us that building something and actually evaluating it are two very different experiences. We designed Sakay Partido with commuters in mind, but it wasn't until we sat down with the Nielsen Norman heuristics and walked through each feature one by one that we truly understood where our design fell short. Small things we had overlooked turned out to matter a lot to real users. We also learned that usability isn't the job of just one person on the team; every role, from documenter to designer to data gatherer, contributed a different lens that made our evaluation richer and more honest. This study didn't just help us improve Sakay Partido, it changed the way we think about every digital product we'll design or use in the future.
